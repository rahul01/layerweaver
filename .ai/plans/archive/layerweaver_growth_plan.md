## The Brand Identity (What You Actually Are)

Your data reveals a consistent identity: **animated, tactile, slightly dark decorative objects for people who find normal decor boring.**

Ghost lamp. T-rex skeleton. Wobble penguin. Floating cat. Night dragon. Articulated spider. These aren't random SKUs — they're from the same universe: playful, slightly weird, well-made things for people aged 22–38 who want their desk to say something about them. They buy these as self-gifts or unusual presents. They found you through a reel that stopped their thumb or a WhatsApp forward.

They are not searching "3D printed products." They're searching "ghost lamp," "cool desk toy," or reacting to a video.

## The Plan

### 1. Fix the Traffic Problem First

This is the entire business problem right now. 800 sessions/month is not a business — it's a prototype. To grow you need to be visible to more people who are already your customer.

**Organic Instagram (zero cost, highest leverage):**

Three content pillars only. Don't deviate.

- **Product in motion (60%)** — Reels under 15 seconds. Ghost lamp glowing in a dark room. Skeleton articulating. Penguin wobbling. No voiceover. Satisfying ambient sound. The product sells itself when moving. This is your reel content.
- **Product in a real space (25%)** — Lifestyle shots: lamp on an actual desk, next to a real laptop, in someone's room. Not studio. Not white background. Makes the viewer imagine it in their space. This drives saves and DMs.
- **Making it (15%)** — Print coming off the printer, painting, packing orders. Authenticity content. Builds trust and following. 30–60 seconds.

**Stop posting:** products laid flat on a plain background. Workshop/Pune-specific content. Collaborations with unrelated accounts. These cost you attention without earning sales.

**Bio rewrite:**
```
Animated desk toys & lamps for people with weird taste 🖤
Ghost lamps · T-rex skeletons · Kinetic desk art
Ships pan-India · DM for custom orders
```

**The one post to make this week:** Ghost balloon lamp glowing in a dark room. Product moving. No text. Pin it to the top of your profile. Everything else follows from there.

**WhatsApp/word of mouth:** 793 of 801 sessions came from somewhere. A significant fraction is probably WhatsApp forwards and DMs. Make it easy for buyers to share — a short note in the packaging saying "tag us or share if you love it" captures this flow deliberately.

### 2. Fix AOV (Current: ₹574 → Target: ₹750+)

The data shows your premium products work — Monstera coaster at ₹1,046, Octopus lamp at ₹1,355, Stegosaurus at ₹549, Brachiosaurus at ₹482. The floor is being set by Spider at ₹189 and T-rex at ₹297. You need to shift the mix.

**Three bundles to create:**

- **"The Haunted Desk"** — Ghost balloon lamp + Kubone Skull Keychain + Articulated Spider. Price at ~₹795 (slight discount from ₹996). Sells your brand identity as a package, not three separate items.
- **"Dino Den"** — T-rex skeleton + Dino box + any articulated dino (Stego or Velociraptor). Price at ~₹995. Targets the collector. Raises T-rex's effective AOV without discounting it.
- **"Lamp Pair"** — Ghost lamp + Snail lamp (or Ghost + Octopus). Gift-oriented. Targets the ₹1,200–₹1,800 gifting occasion. Expose Octopus lamp to more buyers — it's sitting at 2 orders with ₹1,355 AOV and almost no visibility.

**Free shipping threshold:** Set at ₹599. Pushes single ₹399 buyers to add a keychain, bookmark, or coaster to cross the line.

**Upsell at checkout:** Show Skull Keychain or Butterfly Bookmark as add-ons to lamp orders. Low-cost, high-margin, natural pairing.

### 3. Build a Retention Loop

You have no mechanism to bring buyers back. Every sale you make goes cold.

**Minimum viable retention:**
- Instagram: DM every customer who tags you. Reply. This is your relationship surface.
- WhatsApp broadcast: If you're getting mobile numbers at checkout, use them for new product drops and festival bundles. Not spam — one message per month maximum.
- Festival windows: Navratri (Oct 2–11), Dussehra (Oct 12), Diwali (Oct 20). The Ghost lamp and Night Dragon are natural gifting products. Build festival-specific content 3 weeks before each. Rakhi already proved festivals work (Aug 29 was your highest single-day order count).

### 4. Lean Into What Has the Highest Ceiling

Based on 30-day data, these products/categories have the most headroom:

**Articulated Fidgets** (₹50,767 in 30 days, 117 orders) — Your #1 category. The premium dinos (Stego ₹549 AOV, Brachio ₹482) are outperforming T-rex (₹297) per order. Push Stego and Velociraptor in content and ads over T-rex. T-rex is your acquisition product; Stego/Velociraptor are your revenue products.

**Personalization** (Monstera coaster ₹1,046, custom name keyrings ₹303) — 3 orders of Monstera generated ₹5,118. This is a product that nearly no one is discovering organically. One well-placed reel showing the customization process could 10x this. The AOV is your best across all categories.

**Lamps** (₹41,789, 87 orders) — Ghost lamp is carrying this. Octopus lamp at 2 orders with ₹1,355 AOV is invisible. One dedicated reel for Octopus lamp. That's all it needs.

### 5. What Not to Do

- **Don't add more SKUs.** You have 20+ products, most of which are underexposed. Depth beats breadth. The Octopus lamp has 2 orders this month. It needs content, not a product category replacement.
- **Don't discount hero products.** Ghost lamp and T-rex sell at full price. Discounting them trains your audience to wait for sales.
- **Don't run ads without UTM tracking on organic links too.** Your paid ads are tagged. Add UTM to your Instagram bio link (e.g., utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=bio) so you can see organic-driven sessions separately.
- **Don't promote Bunny Keychain as a product.** It's a promotional mechanic (free gift with ₹499+ spend). Keep it as that. Net revenue on 89 orders was ₹252.
