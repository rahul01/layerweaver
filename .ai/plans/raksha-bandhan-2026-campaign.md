## Campaign strategy

**One campaign. Multiple ads. Let Meta pick the winner.**

Don't create separate campaigns per product. Put all creatives into one Raksha Bandhan campaign. Meta will spend more on whichever ad converts best. Your job is to give it 4–6 strong options.

**Campaign name:** Raksha Bandhan 2026
**Budget:** ₹500/day (Aug 1–19) → ₹700/day (Aug 20–28) → ₹200/day (Aug 29–Sep 5)
**Objective:** Sales → Purchase
**Type:** Advantage+ Shopping Campaign
**Duration:** Aug 1 – Sep 5

Keep this campaign completely separate from Hi Value Sales July. Do not add any of these ads inside the existing ad set.

## The creative brief — what to make and how to shoot it

You have 24 hours. Every brief below is designed around a phone camera and natural light. No studio. No equipment.

### Creative 1 — Brother-Sister Photo Frame (Hero Ad)

**Why this is the hero:** It's the only product in your catalog that is *specifically* about the brother-sister relationship. Every other product is a generic gift. This one is Rakhi.

**What to shoot:**
- Print a sample frame (even a test print)
- Insert a casual photo of two people — could be stock, could be you and a sibling, could be a customer's photo if you have one
- Shoot in warm indoor light — this is an emotional product, warm tones work better than cold/clinical
- Two versions: frame on a table (product shot), and someone holding it toward the camera (human connection shot)

**Hook options — test all 3, pick best:**

| Hook | Opening visual | Opening line |
|---|---|---|
| A — Emotion first | Close-up of the photo frame with a real photo inside | "She's had a thousand gifts. She'll remember this one." |
| B — Process reveal | Hands holding a freshly printed frame, still warm | "3D printed in Pune. Her name on it. Ships in 3 days." |
| C — Scroll-stopper | Black screen, then frame glows into view (if backlit) | "What do you give a sister who has everything?" |

**Copy for this ad:**

> What do you give a sister who has everything?
>
> A LayerWeaver brother-sister photo frame — personalised, 3D printed in Pune, made to last. Not something she'll put in a drawer.
>
> Ships across India. Free shipping above ₹299. Order by August 24 for Raksha Bandhan delivery.

**Headline:** "A Gift She'll Actually Keep"
**Format:** 1:1 or 4:5 image for feed. If you can do a 15s Reel of the frame being held + photo inserted, that's the best version.

### Creative 2 — Custom Name Keyring (Volume Ad)

**Why this works:** ₹149 is the lowest-friction gifting product in your catalog. "I'll put your name on something" is a universally understood gifting gesture. The emotional connection is instant. High click volume even if CPP math is tricky — use it to drive traffic, upsell on the page.

**What to shoot:**
- 2–3 keyrings on a flat surface with different names (use names like "Priya," "Arjun," "Ananya" — common, relatable)
- One keyring being held in someone's hand (adds human element)
- Option: one keyring on a set of keys, another wrapped with a small ribbon next to it

**Hook options:**

| Hook | Opening visual | Opening line |
|---|---|---|
| A — Name reveal | Extreme close-up of the name on the keyring, pull back to show full product | "Her name. On something she'll carry every day." |
| B — Sibling angle | Two keyrings side by side — one name each | "One for you. One for her. Raksha Bandhan sorted." |
| C — Simple direct | Flat lay of 3 keyrings with different names | "Custom name keyrings. ₹149. Ships in 3 days." |

**Copy for this ad:**

> This Raksha Bandhan, put their name on something they'll actually use.
>
> Custom 3D printed name keyrings from LayerWeaver — personalised, made in Pune, ships across India.
>
> ₹149. Free shipping above ₹299. Order by August 24 to arrive before Raksha Bandhan.

**Headline:** "Put Their Name On It. ₹149."
**Format:** 1:1 image. Can also do a 3-image carousel showing different names/colours if you have variations.

### Creative 3 — Ghost Balloon Lamp (Wow-Factor Ad)

**Why this works:** Not a "Rakhi product" by nature — but it's your best visual asset and converts well. Position it as "the gift they didn't know they wanted." Works for both genders — brother giving sister, sister giving brother.

**What to shoot:**
- Low light room, lamp on — the glow does all the work
- Two shots: lamp off in daylight (looks unremarkable), lamp on in dark (wow)
- The contrast between these two frames IS the creative — before/after

**Hook options:**

| Hook | Opening visual | Opening line |
|---|---|---|
| A — Reveal | Lamp off, lights on → cut to lamp on, lights off | "It doesn't look like much. Until you turn off the lights." |
| B — Reaction | Someone's face seeing it glow for the first time | "This is what their face looked like." |
| C — Gifting context | Lamp in a simple gift bag / box, then opened and glowing | "The gift that needs no explanation." |

**Best format for this: 15-second Reel.** You can shoot it in under 10 minutes. Film the lamp off, then film the lamp on in a darkened room. Edit together with a text overlay. No voiceover needed.

**Copy for this ad:**

> The ghost balloon lamp is 3D printed in Pune. It looks normal in daylight.
>
> Turn off the lights.
>
> ₹399. Ships across India in 3 days. Free shipping above ₹299.
>
> Order by August 24 for Raksha Bandhan delivery.

**Headline:** "The Gift That Glows"
**Format:** Reel (9:16, 15s) is best. Square image as fallback.

### Creative 4 — Sweeping Sign Nameplate (Premium Gift Ad)

**Why this works:** ₹899 is the highest-margin product in this group. Targets the buyer who wants to give something meaningful and permanent — older sibling, working professional, someone who can spend more.

**What to shoot:**
- Nameplate on a desk or shelf with a name on it
- Warm light, clean background — this is a premium product, the photo should feel premium
- Angle shot (not straight-on) makes it look more 3D and impressive
- Optional: small plant or book nearby to show "desk context"

**Hook options:**

| Hook | Opening visual | Opening line |
|---|---|---|
| A — Permanence | Nameplate on a desk, pull focus | "Her name. On her desk. Forever." |
| B — Premium angle | Close-up of the engraved/printed name, high detail | "Not a keychain. Something she puts on her desk." |
| C — Contrast | Next to a generic gift bag: "everyone gives this" / nameplate: "you could give this" | "Skip the chocolates this Raksha Bandhan." |

**Copy for this ad:**

> Some gifts get put in a drawer. This one goes on her desk.
>
> The LayerWeaver Sweeping Sign Nameplate — personalised with her name, 3D printed in Pune. A gift that stays.
>
> ₹899. Ships across India. Free shipping above ₹299. Order by August 22 for Raksha Bandhan delivery.

**Headline:** "Her Name. On Her Desk. Forever."
**Format:** 1:1 image. Alternatively, a slow zoom-in video (3–5 seconds) edited in Canva with the nameplate as hero.

### Creative 5 — The Bundle Ad (Cable Name Tag + Name Clicker as add-ons)

**Why this works:** The cable name tag and name clicker are below ad break-even solo — but as part of a "desk gift bundle" they add value without driving the CPP math.

**Concept:** Position 2–3 products together as a "Rakhi gift set." Ghost Lamp + Name Keyring + Cable Name Tag as a desk/room set. Or Name Clicker + Name Keyring as a "stationery for her" set.

**What to shoot:**
- Flat lay: all 3 products arranged neatly together on a clean surface
- Name consistent across all products (e.g. "Ananya" on the keyring, "Ananya" on the cable tag, "Ananya" on the clicker)
- Shoot from directly above

**Copy for this ad:**

> A Raksha Bandhan set she'll use every day.
>
> Custom name keyring · Cable name tag · Name clicker — all personalised, all made in Pune.
>
> Order as a set or mix and match. Ships in 3 days. Free shipping above ₹299.
>
> Order by August 24 for Raksha Bandhan delivery.

**Headline:** "The Personalised Gift Set. All Her Name."
**Format:** 1:1 image, flat lay style.

## Shooting schedule — get this done today

**Setup needed:** Natural window light or open shade outdoors. White/cream surface (bedsheet, poster board). Your phone camera.

| Time block | What to shoot |
|---|---|
| Morning light (8–10 AM) | Photo frame hero shots — warm morning light is perfect |
| Midday | Keyring flat lays — needs bright, even light |
| Late afternoon | Nameplate angle shots — soft afternoon light |
| Evening / night | Ghost Lamp glow shots — needs low/no ambient light |
| Any time | Bundle flat lay (once other products are arranged) |

**Total time needed:** 2–3 hours shooting + 1–2 hours editing in Canva or similar.

|---|---|---|
| Aug 1–14 | Low — awareness | "Shop Rakhi gifts →" |
| Aug 15–21 | Medium — "don't forget" | "Order before it's too late →" |

Swap the primary text on existing ads when you hit each phase — but note that changing ad copy is a significant edit and will trigger a brief learning disruption. Do these changes at phase transitions, not daily.

## What not to do

**Don't run this inside Hi Value Sales July.** Full reset on a recovering campaign.

**Don't create a separate ad set per product.** ₹500/day across 5 ad sets = ₹100/day each. That's not enough to exit learning on any of them. One ad set, all products.

**Don't judge performance in the first 7 days.** Learning phase. CPP will be ugly.

**Don't increase budget before Aug 15.** Let it learn at ₹500/day first.

**Don't run the Pixar lamp or Silk Song accessories** in this campaign or any paid campaign. IP flags will get the campaign paused, which disrupts the whole Raksha Bandhan push.
