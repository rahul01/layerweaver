**All discounts auto-apply at checkout — never ask customers to enter a code in ad copy.** Just mention the benefit.

The free bunny keychain is a powerful gifting hook for Rakhi — "free gift with every order above ₹499" resonates strongly in a gifting context. Lead with this in Little Things and Kid Sibling ads where AOV is lower.

Free shipping above ₹299 should appear in every ad — it removes a key objection for small orders.


**Keep this completely separate from Hi Value Sales July.** Don't add any of these ads to the existing campaign. Different objective, different audience moment, different landing pages.

**Why these 4 first:** Gamer Bro and Aesthetic Sister are the most specific personas — specificity = high relevance = lower CPM. Little Things covers everyone who just wants something under ₹500. Kid Sibling captures a segment nobody else is targeting in Rakhi ads.

Add The WFH Sister and Desk Setup Bro in Week 2 if budget allows — they share products with other collections so let Meta figure out which persona framing converts better first.

## Making the creatives — no fancy tools needed

Each ad is a **Canva collage** of 3–4 product images from that collection. 15–20 minutes per ad.

**How to make each one:**

1. Open Canva → New design → 1080×1080 (feed) or 1080×1920 (Reels/Stories)
2. Create a grid with 3–4 product images from the collection
3. Add persona label as headline: e.g. "For the Gamer Bro 🎮"
4. Add offer line: "Use code RAKHI10 for 10% off"
5. LayerWeaver logo small at bottom
6. Export JPG

Product images are on your Shopify CDN — open each product page and download directly.

**Tips:**
- White or cream background unifies products that have different photo styles
- Headline text: minimum 40pt, readable while scrolling
- Don't over-design — 4 products + headline + code + logo is enough
- Make one 1:1 and one 9:16 version per ad (the 9:16 runs on Reels/Stories automatically)

## What not to do

**Don't add these ads into Hi Value Sales July.** That campaign is recovering at ₹155 CPP. Adding new ads resets learning. Full stop.

**Don't link to the homepage or all-products page.** The entire value of persona collections is that the landing page already does the selling. Link directly to each collection.

**Don't run all 9 collections at once.** ₹700/day across 9 ad sets = ₹77/day each — not enough for any ad to exit learning. Four ads in one ad set, let Meta allocate.

**Don't judge by day 1–3 CPP.** Learning phase CPP is always ugly. Give it 7 days minimum before any decisions.

**Don't use FAMILY15 in ads.** It's a relationship discount for custom/repeat customers, not a broadcast offer.
